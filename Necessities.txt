For **one room controller**, based on everything we've discussed, here's a complete hardware list.

# Main Controller

* 1 × ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (recommended) *(or ESP32-WROOM-32 if you already have it)*

# Motor Control

* 4 × TB6612FNG Dual H-Bridge Modules *(controls 8 DC motors total: 4 doors + 4 windows)*

# Door & Window Motors

* 4 × DC geared motors (Doors)
* 4 × DC geared motors (Windows)

# Position Detection

* 16 × Hall Effect Sensors *(2 per door/window: Open & Closed)*
* 16 × Neodymium Magnets

# Lighting

* 6 × Relay Channels *(or MOSFET channels if dimming is required)*

# Appliances

* 8 × Relay Channels

# Cameras

* 3 × Relay/MOSFET Channels *(for camera power or reset control)*

# Intrusion Detection

* Up to 5 × Panasonic EKMC1601111 PIR Motion Sensors

# Authentication

* 1 × Fingerprint Sensor (e.g., R503 or AS608)
* 1 × RFID Reader (e.g., MFRC522)

# Status Indicators

* 5 × LEDs
* 5 × 220Ω Resistors

Suggested LED functions:

* Power
* Wi-Fi
* MQTT
* Armed/Disarmed
* Alarm/Fault

# GPIO Expansion

* 3 × MCP23017 (16 GPIO I²C Expanders)

Outputs → MCP23017
Inputs → 74HC4067

# Power

* 1 × 12V DC Power Supply
* 1 × 5V Buck Converter
* 1 × 3.3V Regulator *(if needed for your PCB design)*

# Protection

* Fuses
* Flyback Diodes *(if using bare relay coils)*
* Reverse Polarity Protection
* TVS Diodes
* Decoupling Capacitors

# Connectors

* Screw Terminal Blocks
* JST Connectors (optional)
* Pin Headers

# Communication

* Wi-Fi (ESP32 built-in)
* MQTT (software)
* HTTP (for initial provisioning if required)

# PCB Components

* PCB
* Crystal/Oscillator (if designing from the ESP32 module reference)
* Reset Button
* Boot Button
* Programming Header (USB-UART or native USB depending on the ESP32 module)

# Storage

* SPI Flash *(built into ESP32 module)*
* NVS (for storing Hardware ID, Auth Key, JWT, and configuration)
